Strict-Transport-Security: max-age at least 31536000, includeSubDomains. Forces HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'. Blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff. Stops content-type guessing.

Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin. Full path not leaked to other sites.

X-Frame-Options: DENY, or frame-ancestors 'none' inside Content-Security-Policy. Against clickjacking.

Permissions-Policy: camera, microphone, geolocation off, if the site does not use them.

Omit X-XSS-Protection: deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, older versions introduced vulnerabilities.
