Eventual consistency: after write, replicas do not agree immediately, but converge within finite time if writes stop.

Trade against availability. System demanding strong consistency has to refuse requests when replicas cannot talk.

Why care: it bites whoever reads right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, does not see it. Common fix: read from primary replica for session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Convergence time within same region typically under a second; across regions rises to seconds.

Not defect to eliminate but choice: most systems that scale accept it deliberately. Cases where unacceptable few and recognisable: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of identifier.
