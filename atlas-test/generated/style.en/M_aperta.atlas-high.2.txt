Before architecture, two things about the monolith itself.

Which part actually changes. Stable parts gain nothing from being split.

Where transaction boundaries sit. Transaction spanning two services becomes distributed problem.

Do not plan full rewrite running parallel to old system: almost always fails, old one keeps changing, copy never catches up.

Instead, progressive strangling: router in front, one piece moved at a time, old system authoritative until new one verified.

Microservices cost more in operations: each service wants releases, monitoring, logs, alerts, person looking after it. Below five developers, operational cost almost always exceeds benefit.

Option nobody mentions: **modular monolith**. Sharp internal boundaries, one release, extract module the day genuinely needed.

Deciding question not technical: what must team be able to do a year from now that it cannot do today.
