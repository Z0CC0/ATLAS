Before choosing architecture, find out which parts of monolith actually change. Stable parts gain nothing from being split, so leave them.

Then locate transaction boundaries. Transaction spanning two services becomes distributed problem, not refactor.

Do not run full rewrite in parallel with old system. Old one keeps changing, copy never catches up, approach almost always fails. Strangle progressively instead: router in front, move one piece at a time, old system stays authoritative until new piece verified.

Count operational cost before picking microservices. Every service wants releases, monitoring, logs, alerts and person looking after it. Below five developers, cost almost always exceeds benefit.

Option nobody mentions: modular monolith. Sharp internal boundaries, one release, extract module day it genuinely needed.

Deciding question not technical: what must team be able to do year from now that it cannot do today. Answer that first, architecture follows.
