Postgres.

Small project: `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` enough up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute. Job sits in same transaction as data, so never starts while matching write rolled back. Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, latency under a millisecond. Cost: one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence not automatic: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.
