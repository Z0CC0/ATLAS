After a write, replicas disagree briefly, then converge within finite time once writes stop.

Trade against availability: strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas cannot talk.

Why care: you post comment, reload, comment missing. Hits whoever reads right after writing.

Common fix: session that just wrote reads from primary replica, not everyone.

Convergence same region under a second; across regions, seconds.

Not defect, choice: most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of identifier.
