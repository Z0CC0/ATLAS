Postgres, for small project. Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands.

Postgres with SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED enough up to roughly thousand jobs per minute. Job sits in same transaction as data: job can never start while matching write rolled back. Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, latency under millisecond. Cost: one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence must be configured: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
