Postgres.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED enough up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data: never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, latency under a millisecond. One more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence must be configured: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.
