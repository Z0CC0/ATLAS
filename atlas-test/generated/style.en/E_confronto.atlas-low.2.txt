Postgres.

At small-project volume, `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` is enough, up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data: if write rolls back, job never starts. Redis cannot give that.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there. Redis needs AOF enabled, or restart loses queued jobs.

Redis also means one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Its advantage, far more volume and sub-millisecond latency, only matters once volume genuinely demands it. Move then, not before.
