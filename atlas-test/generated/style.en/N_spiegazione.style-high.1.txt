Replicas disagree right after a write, converge within finite time once writes stop.

Trade against availability: strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas cannot talk.

Bites whoever reads right after writing: user posts comment, reloads, comment missing.

Common fix: session that just wrote reads from primary replica, not everyone.

Convergence same region usually under a second, across regions seconds.

Not a defect, a choice: most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
