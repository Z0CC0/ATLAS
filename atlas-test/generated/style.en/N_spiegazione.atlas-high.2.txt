After write, replicas disagree for a while, then converge within finite time once writes stop.

Trade against availability: strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas cannot talk.

Why care: whoever reads right after writing. Post comment, reload, comment missing.

Common fix: session that just wrote reads from primary replica, not everyone.

Convergence usually under a second within one region, seconds across regions.

Not a defect to eliminate but a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
