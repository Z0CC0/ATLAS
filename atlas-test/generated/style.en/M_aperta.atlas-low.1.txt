Before architecture: find out which part of monolith actually changes. Stable parts gain nothing from being split.

Then locate transaction boundaries. Transaction spanning two services becomes distributed problem.

Full rewrite running in parallel with old system almost always fails: old one keeps changing, copy never catches up. Alternative: progressive strangling. Router in front, move one piece at a time, old system stays authoritative until new one verified.

Microservices cost more in operations: every service wants releases, monitoring, logs, alerts and person who looks after it. Below five developers, operational cost almost always exceeds benefit.

Option nobody mentions: modular monolith. Sharp internal boundaries, one release, and option to extract module the day it genuinely needed.

Deciding question not technical: what must your team be able to do year from now that it cannot do today.
