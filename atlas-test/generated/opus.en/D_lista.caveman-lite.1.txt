For a static site, set these headers:

- **`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`** makes the browser use HTTPS. Use a `max-age` of at least 31536000 seconds (one year).
- **`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'`** blocks injected third-party scripts.
- **`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`** stops the browser from guessing the content type.
- **`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`** stops your full URL path from leaking to other sites.
- **`X-Frame-Options: DENY`** protects against clickjacking. You can instead add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to your Content-Security-Policy.
- **`Permissions-Policy`** turns off camera, microphone and geolocation. Use it if your site doesn't need these features.

Leave out **`X-XSS-Protection`**. It is deprecated, and modern browsers ignore it. Older browser versions that supported it introduced vulnerabilities.

Here is the full set:

```
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains
Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
X-Frame-Options: DENY
Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()
```
