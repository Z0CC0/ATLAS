Set these six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` forces the browser onto HTTPS. Use a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds.

`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'` is the header that blocks third-party script injection.

`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` stops the browser from guessing the content type.

`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` keeps the full path from leaking to other sites.

`X-Frame-Options: DENY` protects against clickjacking. You can instead add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the Content-Security-Policy, which gives `default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'`.

`Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()` turns off features the site doesn't use. Only include the ones you don't need.

Do not set **X-XSS-Protection**. It's deprecated: modern browsers ignore it, and older versions of it introduced vulnerabilities.
