Set these six headers and leave out X-XSS-Protection:

Strict-Transport-Security: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`. This forces the browser onto HTTPS. The max-age should be at least 31536000 seconds, which is one year.

Content-Security-Policy: `default-src 'self'`. This is the header that blocks third-party script injection. Add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the same policy to protect against clickjacking.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY`. This also protects against clickjacking, so you can use it in place of `frame-ancestors 'none'` or alongside it.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. With this, other sites don't receive the full path of your pages.

Permissions-Policy: turn off camera, microphone and geolocation if the site doesn't use them, for example `camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`.

X-XSS-Protection is deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it introduced vulnerabilities.
