Bitcoin came from a 2008 paper, *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*, signed Satoshi Nakamoto. That pseudonym has never been identified.

Project grew out of cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold came close but never solved ordering: agreeing which transaction came first.

Paper's core problem: double spending, meaning same digital money spent twice, without central body keeping ledger. Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting past becomes expensive, because attacker would have to redo proof of work for altered block and every block after it.

First block was generated on 3 January 2009. It contains headline of Times article about bank bailout, which works both as timestamp and as statement of intent.

Issuance follows fixed schedule. Block reward started at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total supply tends to 21 million and never goes beyond it.

First documented commercial transaction came in 2010: two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stopped taking part publicly in 2011. Their bitcoin have never been moved.
