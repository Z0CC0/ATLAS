Bitcoin began with a 2008 paper, *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*, signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified.

It came out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts there, b-money and bit gold, did not solve ordering.

The problem was double spending without a central body keeping the ledger. The solution was a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive because the work must be redone for every later block.

The first block was generated on 3 January 2009. It contains the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which works as both a timestamp and a statement of intent.

The block reward started at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. The total issued tends to 21 million and no further.

The first documented commercial transaction came in 2010: two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stopped taking part publicly in 2011. Those bitcoin have never been moved.
