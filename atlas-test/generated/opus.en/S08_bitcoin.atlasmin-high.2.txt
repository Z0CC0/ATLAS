Bitcoin began in 2008 with a paper signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System".

Roots in the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts like b-money and bit gold never solved transaction ordering.

Problem tackled: double spending without a central body keeping the ledger.

Solution: a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive, because the work would have to be redone for every later block.

First block generated 3 January 2009. It contains the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which works as both a timestamp and a statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total issued tends to 21 million and no further.

First documented commercial transaction in 2010: two pizzas paid for with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stops taking part publicly in 2011. Satoshi's bitcoin have never been moved.
