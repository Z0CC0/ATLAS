internal knowledge

Bitcoin grew out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold never solved how to order transactions.

2008: paper "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System" appears, signed Satoshi Nakamoto. The pseudonym has never been identified.

Problem it tackled: double spending, without any central body keeping the ledger.

Solution: a chain of blocks, ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive because the work would have to be redone for every later block.

3 January 2009: first block generated. It contains the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which serves as both a timestamp and a statement of intent.

Issuance: the block reward started at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total supply approaches 21 million and never goes beyond it.

2010: first documented commercial transaction, two pizzas paid for with 10,000 bitcoin.

2011: Satoshi stopped taking part publicly. Satoshi's bitcoin have never been moved.
