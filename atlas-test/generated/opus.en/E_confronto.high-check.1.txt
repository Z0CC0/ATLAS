internal knowledge

Postgres. Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` is enough up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data: a job can never start while its write is rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, latency under a millisecond. But one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
