internal knowledge

**Postgres.** Switch to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job lives in same transaction as data, so it can never start while its write is rolled back.

Persistence comes free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis wins on volume and latency (under a millisecond), but adds a service to run, monitor and upgrade. Its persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
