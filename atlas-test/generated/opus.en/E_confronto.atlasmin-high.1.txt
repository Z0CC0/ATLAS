**Postgres**, then move to Redis when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job shares transaction with data: job never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, lower latency, under a millisecond.

But one more service to run, monitor, upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
