**Postgres**; switch to Redis only when volume really demands it.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to about a thousand jobs per minute.

Job lives in same transaction as data: never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis: far more volume, latency under a millisecond. But one more service to run, monitor, upgrade, and persistence needs configuring: without AOF, restart loses queued jobs.
