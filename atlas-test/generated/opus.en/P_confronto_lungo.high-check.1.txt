internal knowledge

Local files plus collector shipping them elsewhere. Application never waits on log write.

**Rule that always holds: log write must not sit in request's critical path.**

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. But search means grep, rotation configured by hand or disk fills. No search across machines, you connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. Billed by volume, and at 1000 requests/minute volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure, so personal data must be stripped before sending.

Database: familiar, convenient search. In application's own database, two very different workloads compete. Under load, logs fill caches and connections real requests needed.

Collector setup keeps files' cheap local write and still gets logs off each machine.
