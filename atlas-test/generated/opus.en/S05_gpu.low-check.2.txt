internal knowledge

Main difference: RTX 5080 pulls ahead in frame generation, not raw rasterisation, where real gain sits around 10-15 per cent.

Architecture: 5080 built on Blackwell, 4080 on Ada Lovelace.

Memory: GDDR7 on 5080 versus GDDR6X on 4080, with bandwidth rising from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Power: rated draw goes from 320 W to 360 W, but connector stays 16-pin, so same cable works.

If you already own a 4080 and play mostly without frame generation, upgrade brings modest gains.
