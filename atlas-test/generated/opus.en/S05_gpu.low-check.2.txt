internal knowledge

RTX 5080 gains the most in frame generation. In raw rasterisation it runs only about 10-15 per cent faster than the RTX 4080.

Newer architecture: Blackwell on 5080, Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Faster memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X, with bandwidth rising from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Higher power draw: rated at 360 watts instead of 320.

Power connector unchanged: both use 16 pins.
