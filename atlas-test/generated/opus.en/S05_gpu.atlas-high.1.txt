The 5080's biggest gain over the 4080 is in frame generation, not raw rasterisation.

**Rasterisation:** about 10–15 per cent faster.

**Architecture:** Blackwell on the 5080, Ada Lovelace on the 4080.

**Memory:** GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

**Power:** rated draw rises from 320 to 360 W. Both cards use the same 16-pin connector.
