internal knowledge

Bitcoin grew out of cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold had not solved ordering: agreeing which transaction came first.

In 2008 paper appears, signed Satoshi Nakamoto, pseudonym never identified. Title: Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System. Problem it tackles: double spending, without central body keeping ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Proof of work makes rewriting past expensive, since attacker would have to redo it for every later block.

First block generated on 3 January 2009. It contains headline of Times article about bank bailout. That reference works both as timestamp and as statement of intent.

Issuance fixed from start. Block reward begins at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. So total issued tends to 21 million and no further.

First documented commercial transaction comes in 2010: two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stops taking part publicly in 2011. Satoshi's bitcoin have never been moved.
