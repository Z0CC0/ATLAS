internal knowledge

Bitcoin grew out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold never solved ordering: agreeing which transaction came first.

In 2008 a paper appeared, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto. That is a pseudonym, and the person behind it has never been identified. The problem it tackled was double spending without a central body keeping the ledger.

The solution was a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past becomes expensive, because the work would have to be redone for every later block.

The first block came on 3 January 2009. It contains the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which works both as a timestamp and as a statement of intent.

Issuance follows a fixed schedule. The block reward started at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total supply tends to **21 million, and no further**.

The first documented commercial transaction came in 2010: two pizzas paid for with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stopped taking part publicly in 2011. Their bitcoin have **never been moved**.
