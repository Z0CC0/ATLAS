internal knowledge

Bitcoin came out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts, b-money and bit gold, never solved ordering: agreeing which transaction came first.

2008: paper titled "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified. Problem it tackles: double spending without a central body keeping the ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting past is expensive, since proof of work would have to be redone for every later block.

3 January 2009: first block generated. It contains a Times headline about a bank bailout, serving both as timestamp and as statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

2010: first documented commercial transaction, two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

2011: Satoshi stops taking part publicly. Satoshi's bitcoin have never been moved.
