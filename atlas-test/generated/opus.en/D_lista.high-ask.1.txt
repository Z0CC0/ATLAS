Set these six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection:

`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` makes the browser use HTTPS. The max-age must be at least 31536000 seconds.

`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'` blocks scripts injected from third parties.

`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` stops the browser from guessing the content type.

`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` keeps the full path from leaking to other sites.

`X-Frame-Options: DENY` protects against clickjacking. You can use `frame-ancestors 'none'` inside the Content-Security-Policy instead.

`Permissions-Policy` turns off camera, microphone and geolocation if the site doesn't use them, for example `camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`.

Don't set `X-XSS-Protection`. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older versions introduced vulnerabilities.
