RTX 5080 mostly helps through frame generation. Raw rasterisation gains less.

- **Architecture:** 5080 uses Blackwell. 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.
- **Memory:** 5080 has GDDR7, 4080 has GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.
- **Performance:** biggest jump is frame generation. Rasterisation gain is about 10-15%.
- **Power:** rated draw rises from 320 W to 360 W. Power connector stays at 16 pins.

If your games support frame generation, 5080 is a clear step up. If they don't, expect only a modest gain over 4080 for 40 W more rated power.
