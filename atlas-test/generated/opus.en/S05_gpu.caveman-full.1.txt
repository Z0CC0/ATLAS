RTX 5080 is newer generation. Biggest gain is frame generation, not raw rasterisation.

**Architecture:** 5080 uses Blackwell. 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.

**Memory:** 5080 has GDDR7. 4080 has GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 GB/s to about 960 GB/s.

**Performance:** Frame generation shows the biggest jump. Raw rasterisation gain is only about 10-15%. If you play without frame generation, the 5080 feels like a modest step up, not a new tier.

**Power:** Rated draw rises from 320 W to 360 W. Check that your PSU has headroom for the extra 40 W. Power connector is still 16-pin, so your existing cable fits.

**Upgrade from 4080?** Only worth it if you use frame generation. For pure rasterisation, 10-15% is a small gain for the cost.
