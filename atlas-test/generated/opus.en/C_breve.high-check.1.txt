Usually no. On a column with only three values, a B-tree index is normally not used.

Planner prefers sequential scan once filter selects more than roughly 5-10 per cent of rows. With three values, most filters select more than that.

Index still costs writes and disk space, even when unused.

Exception: partial index on rare value. That one does get used:

```sql
CREATE INDEX orders_failed_idx ON orders (id) WHERE status = 'failed';
```
