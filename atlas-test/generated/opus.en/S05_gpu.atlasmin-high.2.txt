RTX 5080 is a newer generation. Biggest gain is frame generation, not raw rasterisation.

Architecture: Blackwell, up from Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Memory: GDDR7, up from GDDR6X.

Bandwidth: about 717 GB/s up to about 960 GB/s.

Rasterisation: real gain about 10–15 per cent.

Rated power draw: 320 W up to 360 W.

Power connector: still 16-pin.
