**Postgres.** Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job lives in same transaction as data: never starts while matching write rolls back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis: far more volume, latency under a millisecond. Costs one more service to run, monitor, upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
