Postgres. Move to Redis only once volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres:

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job shares transaction with data: never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis:

Far more volume, latency under a millisecond.

One more service to run, monitor, upgrade.

Persistence needs configuring: without AOF, restart loses queued jobs.
