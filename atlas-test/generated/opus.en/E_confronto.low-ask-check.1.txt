internal knowledge

Postgres. Switch to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

With SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, Postgres handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute, enough for a small project.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so job can never start while matching write gets rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume at lower latency, under a millisecond. Price: one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Persistence also needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
