internal knowledge

Set six headers and leave out one.

`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` forces the browser onto HTTPS. Use a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds, which is one year.

`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'` blocks scripts injected from third parties. Of all these headers, it does the most to protect your pages.

`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` stops the browser from guessing the content type of your files.

`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` keeps the full path of your URLs from leaking to other sites.

`X-Frame-Options: DENY` protects against clickjacking. You can instead add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to your Content-Security-Policy, for example `default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'`.

`Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()` turns off the camera, microphone and geolocation. Only add it if your site doesn't use those features.

**Don't set `X-XSS-Protection`.** It's deprecated and modern browsers ignore it. Older browsers that do read it had vulnerabilities caused by it.
