internal knowledge

For a static site, set six headers and leave out a seventh:

Strict-Transport-Security with `max-age` of at least 31536000 seconds and `includeSubDomains`, so the browser always uses HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy with `default-src 'self'`. This is the header that blocks scripts injected from third parties.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`, so the browser doesn't guess the content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, so other sites don't receive the full path.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY` to protect against clickjacking. You can instead add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the Content-Security-Policy you're already sending.

Permissions-Policy to turn off camera, microphone and geolocation, as long as the site doesn't use them.

Leave out X-XSS-Protection. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older versions of it introduced vulnerabilities.
