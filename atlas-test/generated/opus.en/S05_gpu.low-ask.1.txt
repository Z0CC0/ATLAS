RTX 5080 mainly gains in frame generation; in raw rasterisation it's only about 10-15 per cent faster than the 4080.

Architecture changes: 5080 uses Blackwell, 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.

Memory moves from GDDR6X to GDDR7, which raises bandwidth from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Rated power draw rises from 320 to 360 watts. The power connector stays at 16 pins, so the cabling you have for a 4080 still fits.
