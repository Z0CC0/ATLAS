**Postgres**, until volume genuinely demands Redis.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data: job never starts while its matching write is rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, lower latency (under a millisecond). Costs one more service to run, monitor, upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
