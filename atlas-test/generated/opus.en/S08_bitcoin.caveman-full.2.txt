Bitcoin come from cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts like b-money and bit gold existed, but none solved ordering: no one could agree which transaction came first without trusting someone.

**Paper.** In 2008, paper appear: *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*. Signed Satoshi Nakamoto. Name is pseudonym, never identified.

**Problem.** Paper tackle double spending. Digital money is easy to copy, so same coin can be spent twice. Usual fix is central body keeping ledger. Satoshi wanted no central body.

**Solution.** Chain of blocks, ordered by proof of work. Each block cost real computation. To rewrite past, attacker must redo proof of work for that block and for every later block. So history get more expensive to change as chain grows.

**Genesis block.** First block generated 3 January 2009. Block contain headline of Times article about bank bailout. Headline work two ways:
- timestamp: block cannot be older than that day's paper
- statement of intent: new money system born in response to bailed-out banks

**Issuance.** Block reward start at 50 bitcoin. Reward halve every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tend to 21 million and no further. Supply fixed by rule, not by any authority.

**First real use.** First documented commercial transaction in 2010: two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

**Creator exit.** Satoshi stop taking part publicly in 2011. Satoshi's bitcoin never moved since.
