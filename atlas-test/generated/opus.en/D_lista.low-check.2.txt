Internal knowledge.

Set these six headers:

`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` forces the browser onto HTTPS. Use a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds.

`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'` blocks third-party script injection.

`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` stops the browser from guessing the content type.

`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` keeps the full path from leaking to other sites.

`X-Frame-Options: DENY` protects against clickjacking. You can add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the Content-Security-Policy instead.

`Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()` turns off features the site doesn't use. Only include the ones your site doesn't need.

Leave out `X-XSS-Protection`. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older browsers that supported it introduced vulnerabilities.
