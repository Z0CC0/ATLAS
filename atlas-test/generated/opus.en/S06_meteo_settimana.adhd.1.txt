Bring an umbrella on **Wednesday** and **Sunday**. Those are the two days with significant rain in New York this week.

| Day | Sky | High | Low |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Clear | 24° | 17° |
| Tue | Cloudy, light rain in the evening | 22° | 16° |
| Wed | Rain for most of the day | 19° | 15° |
| Thu | Brighter spells | 21° | 14° |
| Fri | Clear | 25° | 16° |
| Sat | Clear and warm | 28° | 19° |
| Sun | Thunderstorms in the afternoon | 26° | 18° |

- **Wednesday** is the coolest day and the wettest one.
- **The weekend is the warmest part of the week.** Saturday is the best day for being outside. On Sunday, plan outdoor things for the morning, before the afternoon storms.
- **Tuesday evening** has only light rain, but a small umbrella is worth carrying if you'll be out late.
