**Postgres.** Move to Redis only once your volume actually needs it.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

The job sits in the same transaction as the data, so a job can never start while its write is being rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if the process dies, the queue is still there.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond. The cost is one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Persistence also has to be configured: without AOF enabled, a restart loses the queued jobs.
