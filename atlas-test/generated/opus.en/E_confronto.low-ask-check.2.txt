internal knowledge

**Postgres.** Switch to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

With `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, Postgres handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute, which covers most small projects.

Job sits in same transaction as your data, so it can never start while its matching write gets rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if the process dies, the queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond. But it's one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Persistence also needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
