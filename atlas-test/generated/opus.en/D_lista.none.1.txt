For a static site, set these six headers and leave out a seventh.

**Set these:**

- **`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`** makes the browser always use HTTPS for your domain and its subdomains. Don't set max-age below 31536000 seconds (one year).
- **`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'`** is your main defence against someone injecting third-party scripts into your pages.
- **`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`** stops the browser from guessing a file's content type. It uses the type you declare.
- **`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`** keeps the full path of your URLs from being sent to other sites.
- **`X-Frame-Options: DENY`** protects against clickjacking. You can get the same effect by adding `frame-ancestors 'none'` to your Content-Security-Policy, for example `default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'`.
- **`Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`** switches off those browser features. Only do this if your site doesn't use them.

**Leave this out:**

- **`X-XSS-Protection`** is deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and older ones had vulnerabilities caused by it, so setting it does nothing useful and can cause harm.
