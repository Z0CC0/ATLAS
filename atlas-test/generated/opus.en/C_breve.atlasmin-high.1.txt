Usually not. On a low-cardinality column, the planner normally ignores a B-tree index.

It picks a sequential scan when the filter matches more than about 5-10% of rows, which is almost always true with three values.

The index still costs extra writes and disk space.

Exception: a partial index on the rare value does get used, e.g. `CREATE INDEX ON orders (status) WHERE status = 'failed';`
