Static site, set these:

- **`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`**: forces the browser onto HTTPS. Use a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds.
- **`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'`**: blocks third-party script injection.
- **`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`**: stops the browser from guessing the content type.
- **`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`**: other sites don't get your full path.
- **`X-Frame-Options: DENY`**, or `frame-ancestors 'none'` in the CSP: protects against clickjacking.
- **`Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`**: turns these off if the site doesn't use them.

Don't set **`X-XSS-Protection`**. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older browser versions that used it introduced vulnerabilities.
