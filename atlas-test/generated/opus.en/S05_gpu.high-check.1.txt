internal knowledge

5080 is a newer generation. Most of its gain comes from frame generation, not raw rasterisation.

Architecture: Blackwell on the 5080, Ada Lovelace on the 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X, with bandwidth rising from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Rasterisation: the real gain is around 10–15%.

Power: rated draw goes from 320 to 360 W, and the power connector is still 16-pin.
