internal knowledge

Main difference: frame generation, not raw rasterisation. Rasterisation gain only about 10-15 per cent.

Architecture: Blackwell on 5080, Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X, bandwidth up from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Power: rated draw up from 320 to 360 W. Connector still 16-pin.
