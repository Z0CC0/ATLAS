internal knowledge

Set these six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

`Strict-Transport-Security`: max-age of at least 31536000 seconds, plus `includeSubDomains`. This forces the browser onto HTTPS.

`Content-Security-Policy`: `default-src 'self'`. This blocks third-party script injection.

`X-Content-Type-Options`: `nosniff`. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

`Referrer-Policy`: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. This keeps the full path from leaking to other sites.

`X-Frame-Options`: `DENY`, which protects against clickjacking. You can use `frame-ancestors 'none'` inside the Content-Security-Policy instead.

`Permissions-Policy`: turn off camera, microphone and geolocation if the site doesn't use them.

**Don't set `X-XSS-Protection`.** It's deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and older versions of it introduced vulnerabilities.
