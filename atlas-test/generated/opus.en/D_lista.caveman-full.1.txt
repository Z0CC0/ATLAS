For static site, set these six headers and skip one old header:

- **`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`** makes the browser use HTTPS. Use a max-age of 31536000 seconds or more.
- **`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'`** blocks injected third-party scripts. Add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the same header to stop clickjacking.
- **`X-Frame-Options: DENY`** also stops clickjacking. You can use it instead of `frame-ancestors 'none'`.
- **`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`** stops the browser from guessing content type.
- **`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`** keeps your full URL path from leaking to other sites.
- **`Permissions-Policy`** turns off camera, microphone and geolocation. Use it if your site doesn't need them.

**Do not send `X-XSS-Protection`.** It is deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it caused vulnerabilities.

Full set:

```
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains
Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'
X-Frame-Options: DENY
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()
```
