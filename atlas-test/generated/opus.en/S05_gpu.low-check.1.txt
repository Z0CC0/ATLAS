internal knowledge

5080 is next generation after 4080, but on plain rasterisation it's only about 10–15 per cent faster. Biggest jump comes from frame generation.

Architecture moves from Ada Lovelace on 4080 to Blackwell on 5080.

Memory changes from GDDR6X to GDDR7, so bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Rated power draw goes from 320 to 360 watts, so check your power supply has headroom.

Power connector doesn't change. Both cards use 16 pins, so the same cable fits.

For games without frame generation, 5080 gives a modest step over 4080. It pulls further ahead in games that support frame generation.
