RTX 5080 is a modest upgrade over 4080: about 10-15 per cent more rasterisation performance. Its biggest jump is in frame generation.

Architecture: Blackwell instead of Ada Lovelace.

Memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Power: rated draw rises from 320 to 360 W. Connector still 16-pin.
