internal knowledge

**Postgres**, for a small project. Switch to Redis only when volume actually requires it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute, likely more than a small project needs.

Job lives in same transaction as data, so a job never starts while its matching write gets rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if the process dies, the queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume with lower latency, under a millisecond. But it means one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Persistence also needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
