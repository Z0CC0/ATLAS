internal knowledge

Set these six headers, and leave out a seventh:

Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains. This makes the browser use HTTPS. Use a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds (one year).

Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'. This is the header that blocks script injected from third parties.

X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin. Other sites won't see the full path of your pages.

X-Frame-Options: DENY protects against clickjacking. You can instead add frame-ancestors 'none' to the Content-Security-Policy.

Permissions-Policy turns off camera, microphone and geolocation. Use it only for features your site doesn't use.

**Don't send X-XSS-Protection.** It's deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and older versions of it introduced vulnerabilities.
