**RTX 5080 vs RTX 4080**

- **Architecture:** 5080 uses Blackwell. 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.
- **Memory:** 5080 has GDDR7. 4080 has GDDR6X. Bandwidth goes from about 717 GB/s to about 960 GB/s.
- **Power:** Rated draw rises from 320 W to 360 W. Same 16-pin connector, so you don't need a new cable.
- **Performance:** The biggest jump is in frame generation. In raw rasterisation the 5080 is only about 10–15% faster.

If you have a 4080 and play mostly without frame generation, the upgrade is small. If you'll use frame generation, the 5080 gains more.
