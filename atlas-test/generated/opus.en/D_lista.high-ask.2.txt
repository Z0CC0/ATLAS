Set six headers and leave out a seventh:

Strict-Transport-Security: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`. This forces the browser onto HTTPS. Use a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds.

Content-Security-Policy: `default-src 'self'`. This blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. This keeps your full path from leaking to other sites.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY`, or add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the Content-Security-Policy instead. Either one protects against clickjacking.

Permissions-Policy: turn off camera, microphone and geolocation if the site doesn't use them. For example: `camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`.

Leave out X-XSS-Protection. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older browsers that did support it introduced vulnerabilities through it.
