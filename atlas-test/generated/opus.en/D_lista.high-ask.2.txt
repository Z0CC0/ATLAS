Set these six headers:

**Strict-Transport-Security**: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`. The max-age should be at least 31536000 seconds. This forces browsers onto HTTPS.

**Content-Security-Policy**: `default-src 'self'`. This is the header that blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. Other sites won't see the full path of your pages.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY`. This protects against clickjacking. You can instead add `frame-ancestors 'none'` inside your Content-Security-Policy.

Permissions-Policy: turn off camera, microphone and geolocation if your site doesn't use them, for example `camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`.

Don't set X-XSS-Protection. It's deprecated: modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it introduced vulnerabilities.
