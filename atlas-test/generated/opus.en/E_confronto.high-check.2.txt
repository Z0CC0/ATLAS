internal knowledge

**Postgres** for a small project. Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job lives in same transaction as data: a job never starts if its write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume, latency under a millisecond.

Redis is one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF, restart loses queued jobs.
