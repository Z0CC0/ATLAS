**Postgres.** Switch to Redis only when volume really needs it.

Why Postgres fits small project:
- `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles roughly 1,000 jobs per minute.
- Job sits in same transaction as data. If matching write rolls back, job never starts.
- Persistence comes free. Process dies, queue survives.

Redis tradeoffs:
- Handles far more volume. Latency under 1 ms.
- Adds one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.
- Persistence needs configuration. Without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Start with Postgres. Move to Redis when sustained load nears ~1,000 jobs per minute or you need sub-millisecond latency.
