RTX 5080 is newer generation, but gain over RTX 4080 is modest outside frame generation.

- **Architecture:** 5080 uses Blackwell. 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.
- **Memory:** 5080 has GDDR7. 4080 has GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 GB/s to about 960 GB/s.
- **Raw performance:** Real rasterisation gain is about 10–15%.
- **Frame generation:** 5080's biggest gain is here, not in rasterisation.
- **Power:** Rated draw rises from 320 W to 360 W. Power connector stays 16-pin, so same cable type works.

**Bottom line:** If you own a 4080, the upgrade pays off mainly when you use frame generation. Without frame generation, expect about 10–15% more speed and a slightly higher power draw.
