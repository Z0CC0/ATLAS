You should set these six headers and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

Strict-Transport-Security: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` (at least 31536000 seconds). This makes the browser use HTTPS for your domain and all its subdomains.

Content-Security-Policy: `default-src 'self'`. This blocks scripts injected from third-party sources. You can also add `frame-ancestors 'none'` here to stop other sites from framing your pages.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`. This stops the browser from guessing a file's content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. Other sites see only your origin, not the full path of the page.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY`. This prevents clickjacking. If you already set `frame-ancestors 'none'` in the CSP, you can skip it.

Permissions-Policy: turn off camera, microphone and geolocation if the site doesn't use them, for example `camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`.

Don't send X-XSS-Protection. It's deprecated, so modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it introduced vulnerabilities.
