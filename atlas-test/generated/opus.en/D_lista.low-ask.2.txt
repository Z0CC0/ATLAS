Set six headers and leave out a seventh:

Strict-Transport-Security with a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds and includeSubDomains. This forces the browser to use HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy with default-src 'self'. This header blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, so the browser doesn't guess the content type.

Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin, so the full path doesn't leak to other sites.

X-Frame-Options: DENY to protect against clickjacking. You can instead add frame-ancestors 'none' to your Content-Security-Policy.

Permissions-Policy to turn off camera, microphone and geolocation, as long as your site doesn't use them.

**Don't send X-XSS-Protection.** It's deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and older browsers that supported it introduced vulnerabilities through it.

```
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains
Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
X-Frame-Options: DENY
Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()
```
