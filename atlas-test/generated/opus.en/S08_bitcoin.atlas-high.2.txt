Bitcoin came out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts like b-money and bit gold never solved transaction ordering.

In 2008 a paper appeared, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto. That is a pseudonym, never identified.

Its problem: double spending, with no central body keeping the ledger.

Its solution: a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive, because the work would have to be redone for every later block.

The first block came on 3 January 2009. It carries the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which works as both a timestamp and a statement of intent.

The block reward started at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total supply tends to 21 million and never goes beyond it.

The first documented purchase came in 2010: two pizzas, paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stopped taking part publicly in 2011. Their bitcoin have never been moved.
