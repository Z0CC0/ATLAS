**Eventual consistency:** after a write, replicas disagree for a while, then converge within finite time once writes stop.

**Why care:** someone reading right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, comment missing.

Trade against availability: strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas cannot talk.

Convergence: under a second across replicas in same region, seconds across regions.

Common fix: read from primary replica for session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Not a defect to eliminate but a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
