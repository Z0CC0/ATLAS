The RTX 5080 is a generational step up from the 4080. Most of the gain comes from frame generation, not raw performance.

- **Architecture:** the 5080 uses Blackwell. The 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.
- **Memory:** the 5080 has GDDR7 and the 4080 has GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 GB/s to about 960 GB/s.
- **Performance:** frame generation is where the 5080 pulls furthest ahead. In plain rasterisation, expect only about 10-15% more.
- **Power:** rated draw goes from 320 W to 360 W. The 16-pin power connector stays the same, so the cable type does not change.

If you already own a 4080 and mostly play without frame generation, a 10-15% gain is modest. If you play titles that use frame generation, the 5080 is a bigger step up.
