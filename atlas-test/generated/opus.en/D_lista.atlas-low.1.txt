Set six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

Strict-Transport-Security: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` at minimum. This makes the browser use HTTPS for your domain and all its subdomains.

Content-Security-Policy: `default-src 'self'`. This blocks scripts injected from third-party origins.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`. This stops the browser from guessing a file's content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. Other sites see only your origin, not the full path of the page.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY`. This protects against clickjacking. You can instead add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the Content-Security-Policy.

Permissions-Policy: if the site doesn't use the camera, microphone or geolocation, turn them off, for example `camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`.

Don't send X-XSS-Protection at all. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it created vulnerabilities.

For example:

```
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains
Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
X-Frame-Options: DENY
Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()
```
