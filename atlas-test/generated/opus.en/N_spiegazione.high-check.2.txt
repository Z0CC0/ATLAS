internal knowledge

Eventual consistency: after write, replicas disagree for a while, but converge within finite time once writes stop.

Price paid for availability. Strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas cannot talk to each other.

Who it hurts: whoever reads right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, does not see it.

Common fix: read from primary replica for the session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Convergence usually under one second across replicas in the same region. Across regions, rises to seconds.

Not a defect to eliminate. A choice: most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, identifier uniqueness.
