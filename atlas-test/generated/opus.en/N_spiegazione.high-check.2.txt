internal knowledge

Eventual consistency: after write, replicas disagree for a while, then converge within finite time once writes stop.

Why care: user posts comment, reloads, comment missing. Hits whoever reads right after writing.

Convergence: usually under one second between replicas in same region. Across regions, seconds.

Not a defect to eliminate. It's a choice, and most systems that scale accept it deliberately. Trade is against availability: system demanding strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas cannot talk.

Common fix: send reads to primary replica only for session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Unacceptable in few cases, easy to spot: account balances, seat availability, identifier uniqueness.
