Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas don't agree immediately, but they converge within a finite time once writes stop.

Why you'd care: someone posts a comment, reloads, and doesn't see it. It only bites whoever reads right after writing.

Fix: read from the primary replica for the session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Lag: under a second across replicas in the same region, seconds across regions.

Trade is against availability. Demanding strong consistency means refusing requests when replicas can't talk.

Not a defect to eliminate but a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
