Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas don't agree right away, but they converge within finite time once writes stop.

Why care: your user posts a comment, reloads, and doesn't see it. Anyone reading right after writing gets hit.

Convergence: usually under a second between replicas in the same region, seconds across regions.

Fix: the session that just wrote reads from the primary replica. Everyone else doesn't need to.

Trade is against availability: a system demanding strong consistency has to refuse requests when replicas can't talk.

Not a defect to eliminate but a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in a few recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, identifier uniqueness.
