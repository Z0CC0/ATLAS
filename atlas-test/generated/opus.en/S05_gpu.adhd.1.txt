**Bottom line:** the RTX 5080 is about 10–15% faster than the 4080 in normal rendering (rasterisation). Its biggest advantage is frame generation.

**What changed**

| | RTX 4080 | RTX 5080 |
|---|---|---|
| Architecture | Ada Lovelace | Blackwell |
| Memory type | GDDR6X | GDDR7 |
| Memory bandwidth | ~717 GB/s | ~960 GB/s |
| Rated power draw | 320 W | 360 W |
| Power connector | 16-pin | 16-pin (same) |

**What this means for you**

1. **Performance:** if your games don't use frame generation, expect a modest 10–15% gain over the 4080.
2. **Frame generation:** this is where the 5080 pulls clearly ahead. It's the main reason to choose it.
3. **Power:** it draws 40 W more. Your current power supply may need checking, but the 16-pin cable stays the same.

**Next step:** check whether the games you play most support frame generation. If they do, the 5080 is the stronger buy. If they don't, the 4080 is close enough.
