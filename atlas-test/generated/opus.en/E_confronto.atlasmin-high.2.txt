**Postgres.** Move to Redis only when volume really demands it.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to about a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data: job never starts while matching write rolls back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume, latency under a millisecond, but adds one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
