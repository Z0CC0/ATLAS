Postgres, for a small project. Switch to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Jobs share a transaction with your data, so a job never starts while its matching write gets rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if the process dies, the queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume at lower latency, under a millisecond.

It's also one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Its persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
