internal knowledge

5080 is mostly a frame-generation upgrade. Raw rasterisation only about 10-15 per cent faster.

Architecture: Blackwell, not Ada Lovelace.

Memory: GDDR7, not GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Rated power: 360 W, up from 320 W.

Power connector: still 16-pin.
