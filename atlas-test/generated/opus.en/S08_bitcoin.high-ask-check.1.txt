internal knowledge

Bitcoin grew out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts, b-money and bit gold, did not solve ordering.

In 2008 a paper appeared, signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System".

Problem: double spending without a central body keeping the ledger.

Solution: a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive, since the work would have to be redone for every later block.

First block generated 3 January 2009. It carries the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which serves as both a timestamp and a statement of intent.

Block reward started at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction, 2010: two pizzas for 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stopped taking part publicly in 2011. Those bitcoin have never been moved.
