internal knowledge (not checked against other sources, as you asked)

5080 is a modest step up from 4080, not a big leap. Biggest gain is frame generation; raw rasterisation is only about 10-15 per cent faster.

Architecture: Blackwell on 5080, Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X, bandwidth up from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Power: rated draw rises from 320 to 360 watts. Power connector is still 16-pin.
