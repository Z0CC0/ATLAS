internal knowledge

RTX 5080 mainly better at frame generation; raw rasterisation gain only about 10-15 per cent.

Architecture: Blackwell on 5080, Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 on 5080, GDDR6X on 4080; bandwidth up from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Power: rated draw up from 320 to 360 W; connector still 16-pin.
