internal knowledge

Bitcoin grew out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold did not solve ordering, meaning agreement on which transaction came first.

In 2008 a paper appeared, signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified. It was titled *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*. It tackled double spending without a central body keeping the ledger.

Its solution was a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive, because the proof of work would have to be redone for every later block.

The first block was generated on 3 January 2009. It contains the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which works both as a timestamp and as a statement of intent.

The block reward started at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total issuance tends to 21 million and goes no further.

The first documented commercial transaction came in 2010: two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stopped taking part publicly in 2011. Satoshi's bitcoin have never been moved.
