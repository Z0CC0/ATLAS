internal knowledge

Eventual consistency: after write, replicas disagree for a while, then converge within finite time, provided writes stop.

Why care: it hits whoever reads right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, comment missing.

Common fix: read from primary replica for the session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Convergence: usually under a second across replicas in same region; seconds across regions.

Trade is against availability: strong consistency means refusing requests when replicas cannot talk.

Not a defect to eliminate but a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, identifier uniqueness.
