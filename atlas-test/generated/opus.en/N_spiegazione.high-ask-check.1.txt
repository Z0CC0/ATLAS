internal knowledge

Replicas don't agree right after a write, but converge within finite time once writes stop.

Why care: user posts comment, reloads, doesn't see it. Hits whoever reads right after writing.

Trade is against availability. Strong consistency means refusing requests when replicas can't talk to each other.

Convergence: typically under a second across replicas in same region, seconds across regions.

Common fix: read from primary replica for the session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Not a defect to eliminate. A choice: most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, identifier uniqueness.
