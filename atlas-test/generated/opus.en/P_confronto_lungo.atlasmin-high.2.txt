Local files plus a collector that ships them elsewhere, so the application never waits on a log write.

Rule that always holds: writing the log must not sit in the request's critical path.

**Files**: zero cost, no dependency. But searching means grep. Rotation must be configured by hand, or the disk fills. No search across several machines; you connect to each one.

**Managed service**: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. But you pay by volume, and at a thousand requests a minute volume grows fast. Logs leave your own infrastructure, so strip personal data before they go.

**Database**: familiar, convenient searching. But logs in the application's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections the real requests needed.
