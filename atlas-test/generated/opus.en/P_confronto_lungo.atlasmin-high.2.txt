Local files plus a collector that ships them elsewhere. The application then never waits on a log write.

Rule that always holds: writing the log must not sit in the request's critical path.

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. Search is only grep. Rotation must be configured by hand or the disk fills. There's no search across several machines, so you connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. You pay by volume, and at a thousand requests a minute volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure, so strip personal data before they go.

Database: familiar, convenient search. Logs in the application's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections your real requests needed.
