internal knowledge

Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas don't agree immediately. They converge within finite time if writes stop.

Why it matters to you: someone reading right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, doesn't see it.

Convergence time: under a second across replicas in same region, seconds across regions.

Trade is against availability. System demanding strong consistency has to refuse requests when replicas can't talk.

Not a defect to eliminate. A choice: most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Common fix: read from primary replica for the session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Unacceptable only in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
