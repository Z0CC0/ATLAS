Replicas don't agree right after a write. They converge within a finite time once writes stop.

Same region: usually under a second. Across regions: seconds.

Why it exists: trade against availability. Strong consistency means refusing requests when replicas can't talk to each other.

Why care: it bites whoever reads right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, doesn't see it.

Common fix: read from primary replica for the session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Not a defect to eliminate. A choice: most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, identifier uniqueness.
