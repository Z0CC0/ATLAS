internal knowledge

For ordinary rasterised games the 5080 is only about 10-15 per cent faster than the 4080. Its biggest gain is in frame generation.

The 5080 uses the newer Blackwell architecture, while the 4080 uses Ada Lovelace. It moves from GDDR6X to GDDR7 memory, which raises bandwidth from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Rated power draw rises from 320 to 360 W. The power connector is still 16-pin.
