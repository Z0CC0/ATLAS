internal knowledge

Postgres, for a small project. Switch to Redis when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so a job can never start while its matching write is rolled back.

Persistence free: if process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond.

Cost: one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Persistence must be configured: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
