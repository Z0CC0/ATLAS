internal knowledge

Postgres, for a small project. Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres:

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` enough up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job in same transaction as data, so job never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis:

Far more volume, latency under a millisecond.

One more service to run, monitor, upgrade.

Persistence must be configured: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
