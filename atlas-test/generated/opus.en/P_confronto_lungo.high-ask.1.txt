**Local files plus a collector** that ships them elsewhere, so the application never waits on a log write.

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. But searching means grep, and rotation must be configured by hand or the disk fills. No search across several machines either; you connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. But you pay by volume, and at a thousand requests a minute volume grows fast. Logs also leave your own infrastructure, so personal data must be stripped before they go.

Database: familiar, convenient searching. But logs in the application's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections the real requests needed.

Whatever you pick: **writing the log must never sit in the request's critical path.**
