Local files plus collector shipping them elsewhere. At 1,000 requests/minute, application never waits on log write.

Rule that always holds: log write never in request's critical path.

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. But search means grep, rotation configured by hand or disk fills. No search across several machines; connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. Paid by volume, and at 1,000 requests/minute volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure, so strip personal data before sending.

Database: familiar, convenient search. But logs in application's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections real requests needed.
