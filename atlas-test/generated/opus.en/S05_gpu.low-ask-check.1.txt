internal knowledge

The 5080's biggest gain over the 4080 is in frame generation. On raw rasterisation it's only roughly 10–15 per cent faster.

The 5080 uses the newer Blackwell architecture; the 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.

The 5080 has GDDR7 memory instead of GDDR6X, and bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Rated power draw goes up from 320 to 360 watts, but the power connector is still 16-pin.
