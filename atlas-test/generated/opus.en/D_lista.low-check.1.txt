internal knowledge

Set these six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

Strict-Transport-Security: set `max-age` to at least 31536000 seconds and add `includeSubDomains`. This makes the browser always use HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy: `default-src 'self'`. This is the header that blocks third-party scripts from being injected.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. Other sites won't see the full path of your pages.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY`. This protects against clickjacking. You can also get the same protection by adding `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the Content-Security-Policy.

Permissions-Policy: turn off camera, microphone and geolocation if the site doesn't use them.

Don't send X-XSS-Protection. It's deprecated: modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it introduced vulnerabilities.
