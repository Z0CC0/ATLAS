internal knowledge

For a small project, use Postgres. Switch to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so a job can never start while its matching write gets rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if the process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond. But it means one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Persistence also needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
