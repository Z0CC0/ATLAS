internal knowledge

**Postgres**, at least until volume genuinely demands Redis.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data: job never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, lower latency, under a millisecond. Cost: one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.
