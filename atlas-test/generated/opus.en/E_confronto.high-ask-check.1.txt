internal knowledge

Postgres, for a small project. Move to Redis only when volume really demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job lives in same transaction as data: if the write rolls back, the job never starts.

Persistence comes free. If the process dies, the queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond.

But it adds a service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Its persistence needs configuring. Without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
