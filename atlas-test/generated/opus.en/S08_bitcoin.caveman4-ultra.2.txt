**Roots:** cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts like b-money and bit gold never solved transaction ordering.

**2008:** paper *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System* appears, signed Satoshi Nakamoto. The pseudonym has never been identified.

**Problem:** double spending, with no central body keeping the ledger.

**Solution:** chain of blocks, ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive because the proof of work would have to be redone for every later block.

**3 January 2009:** first block generated. It contains the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which works both as a timestamp and as a statement of intent.

**Issuance:** block reward starts at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total issued tends to 21 million and never goes past it.

**2010:** first documented commercial transaction. Two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

**2011:** Satoshi stops taking part publicly. Satoshi's bitcoin have never been moved.
