The RTX 5080 is a modest step up from the 4080: about 10-15% faster in rasterisation, with the biggest gain in frame generation.

Architecture: Blackwell on the 5080, Ada Lovelace on the 4080.

Memory: the 5080 moves from GDDR6X to GDDR7, raising bandwidth from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Power: rated draw rises from 320 to 360 W, and the power connector is still 16-pin.

If you mostly play without frame generation, the 10-15% gain is the number that matters.
