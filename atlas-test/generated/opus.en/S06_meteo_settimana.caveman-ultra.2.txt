Mostly mild week in New York. Rain on Wednesday and storms on Sunday; the weekend is the warmest part.

- **Monday:** clear, 24°, low 17°
- **Tuesday:** cloudy, 22°, low 16°. Light rain in the evening.
- **Wednesday:** rain for most of the day, 19°, low 15°
- **Thursday:** some sunny spells, 21°, low 14°
- **Friday:** clear, 25°, low 16°
- **Saturday:** clear and warm, 28°, low 19°
- **Sunday:** thunderstorms in the afternoon, 26°, low 18°

Wednesday and Sunday are the only days with heavy rain. Take an umbrella on those days, and on Tuesday evening. Saturday is the best day to be outdoors.
