Set these six headers, and leave X-XSS-Protection out.

`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` makes the browser use HTTPS. The max-age must be at least 31536000 seconds.

`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'` blocks scripts injected from third parties.

`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` stops the browser from guessing the content type.

`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` keeps your full URL path from leaking to other sites.

`X-Frame-Options: DENY` protects against clickjacking. You can instead add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the Content-Security-Policy.

`Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()` turns off camera, microphone and geolocation. Only set this if the site doesn't use them.

Do not set `X-XSS-Protection`. It is deprecated: modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it introduced vulnerabilities.
