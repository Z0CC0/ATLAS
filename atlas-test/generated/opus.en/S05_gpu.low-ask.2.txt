RTX 5080 is a modest step up from the 4080. The biggest gain is in frame generation, while raw rasterisation improves only about 10-15 per cent.

Architecture: 5080 uses Blackwell, 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.

Memory: GDDR7 on 5080, GDDR6X on 4080. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Power: rated draw goes from 320 to 360 watts. Power connector stays at 16 pins, so the same cable fits.

If you mostly play without frame generation, the upgrade from a 4080 is small. The bigger difference shows in games that support frame generation.
