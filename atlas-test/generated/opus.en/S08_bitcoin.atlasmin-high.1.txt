Bitcoin came out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold did not solve ordering.

2008: paper "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified.

Problem: double spending without a central body keeping the ledger. Solution: a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive, because the work would have to be redone for every later block.

3 January 2009: first block, containing the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout. It is both a timestamp and a statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

2010: first documented commercial transaction, two pizzas for 10,000 bitcoin.

2011: Satoshi stops taking part publicly. Those bitcoin have never been moved.
