Bitcoin came from a 2008 paper, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified.

Roots in cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts, b-money and bit gold, did not solve ordering.

Problem: double spending without central body keeping ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting past becomes expensive, since work must be redone for every later block.

First block generated 3 January 2009. It carries headline of Times article on bank bailout, serving as both timestamp and statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin, halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction in 2010: two pizzas for 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stops taking part publicly in 2011. Those bitcoin never moved.
