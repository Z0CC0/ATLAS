**Postgres**, for a small project. Move to Redis when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so a job never starts while its write is rolled back.

Persistence is free: if the process dies, the queue is still there.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond.

But Redis is one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Its persistence also needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses the queued jobs.
