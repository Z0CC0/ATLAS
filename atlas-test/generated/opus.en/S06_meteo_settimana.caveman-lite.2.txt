New York has a mixed week. It starts dry, turns wet midweek, then warms up for the weekend.

- **Monday:** Clear, high 24 degrees, low 17.
- **Tuesday:** Cloudy, high 22, low 16. Light rain arrives in the evening.
- **Wednesday:** Rain for most of the day, high 19, low 15.
- **Thursday:** Brighter spells, high 21, low 14.
- **Friday:** Clear, high 25, low 16.
- **Saturday:** Clear and warm, high 28, low 19.
- **Sunday:** Thunderstorms in the afternoon, high 26, low 18.

The only days with heavy rain are Wednesday and Sunday. Carry an umbrella on those days, and keep one handy on Tuesday evening too. The weekend is the warmest part of the week. Saturday is the best day to be outside. On Sunday, plan outdoor time for the morning, before the storms.
