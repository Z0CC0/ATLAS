Local files plus collector shipping them elsewhere. Application never waits on log write.

Rule always holds: log write never in request's critical path.

Why not files alone: zero cost, no dependency, but search means grep. Rotation configured by hand, or disk fills. No search across several machines; connect to each one.

Why not managed service alone: instant search, alerts, automatic retention, but billed by volume, and at 1,000 requests/minute volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure, so strip personal data before sending.

Why not database: familiar, convenient search, but logs in application's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections real requests needed.
