Local files plus a collector that ships them elsewhere, so the app never waits on a log write.

Rule that always holds: writing logs must stay out of the request's critical path.

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. Search is grep. Rotation must be configured by hand, or the disk fills. No search across several machines; you connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. You pay by volume, and at 1,000 requests a minute volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure, so strip personal data before sending.

Database: familiar, convenient search. Logs in the app's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections that real requests needed.
