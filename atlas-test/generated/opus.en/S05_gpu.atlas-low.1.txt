The RTX 5080 is a modest step up from the RTX 4080. Its biggest gain is in frame generation. In raw rasterisation it's only about 10–15 per cent faster.

**Architecture:** 5080 uses Blackwell, 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.

**Memory:** GDDR7 on the 5080, GDDR6X on the 4080. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

**Power:** rated draw goes up from 320 to 360 watts. Both use a 16-pin power connector, so the cable doesn't change.

If you already own a 4080 and mostly play without frame generation, the upgrade won't feel big. The case for the 5080 rests on frame generation.
