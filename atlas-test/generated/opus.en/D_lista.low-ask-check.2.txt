Internal knowledge, not checked against other sources.

Set these six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

Strict-Transport-Security: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` or a longer max-age. It forces the browser to use HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy: `default-src 'self'`. This is the header that blocks third-party script injection. You can add `frame-ancestors 'none'` to the same policy to cover clickjacking.

X-Content-Type-Options: `nosniff`. It stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. Other sites don't receive the full path of your pages.

X-Frame-Options: `DENY`. It protects against clickjacking. `frame-ancestors 'none'` in the CSP does the same job, so you need one of the two.

Permissions-Policy: if the site doesn't use the camera, microphone or geolocation, turn them off, for example `camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`.

Don't send **X-XSS-Protection**. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it introduced vulnerabilities.
