Mostly dry week in New York. Wednesday and Sunday bring the significant rain, and the weekend is the warmest part.

Monday: clear, high 24 degrees, low 17

Tuesday: cloudy, high 22, low 16, light rain in the evening

Wednesday: rain for most of the day, high 19, low 15

Thursday: some sunny spells, high 21, low 14

Friday: clear, high 25, low 16

Saturday: clear and warm, high 28, low 19

Sunday: thunderstorms in the afternoon, high 26, low 18

Take an umbrella on Wednesday and Sunday afternoon, and possibly on Tuesday evening. Saturday is the best day to be outdoors.
