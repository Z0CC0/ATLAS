RTX 5080 mostly brings better frame generation. On rasterisation it gains only about 10-15 per cent over the 4080.

Architecture moves from Ada Lovelace (4080) to Blackwell (5080).

Memory changes from GDDR6X to GDDR7, and bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Rated power draw goes from 320 to 360 watts. The 16-pin power connector stays the same, so a cable that fits the 4080 also fits the 5080.
