Add these six headers to every response. Leave out `X-XSS-Protection`.

```
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains
Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
X-Frame-Options: DENY
Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()
```

## What each one does

1. **`Strict-Transport-Security`** makes the browser always use HTTPS. Set `max-age` to at least 31536000 seconds (one year) and include `includeSubDomains`.
2. **`Content-Security-Policy`** with `default-src 'self'` blocks scripts injected from third-party sources. `frame-ancestors 'none'` stops other sites from putting your pages in a frame, which protects against clickjacking.
3. **`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`** stops the browser from guessing the content type. It uses the type your server declares.
4. **`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`** sends only your origin to other sites, not the full path.
5. **`X-Frame-Options: DENY`** also protects against clickjacking. You need either this or `frame-ancestors 'none'`. Setting both is harmless.
6. **`Permissions-Policy`** turns off the camera, microphone and geolocation. Only include the features your site doesn't use.

## What to leave out

**`X-XSS-Protection`** is deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it caused vulnerabilities.

Next: add the block above to your web server or host's headers config, deploy, and open DevTools → Network → your page → Response Headers to confirm all six are there.
