Usually not. On a column with three values, the planner normally won't use a B-tree index. When the filter matches more than about 5-10% of the rows, it picks a sequential scan instead. The index still slows down writes and takes disk space, so you pay for it and get nothing back.

There's one exception. If one of the three values is rare and you filter on it often, a partial index on just that value does get used.
