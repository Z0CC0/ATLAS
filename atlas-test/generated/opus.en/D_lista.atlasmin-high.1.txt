Set these six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` makes the browser always use HTTPS. The max-age should be at least 31536000 seconds.

`Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'` blocks third-party scripts from being injected into your pages.

`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` stops the browser from guessing the content type.

`Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` keeps the full path from leaking to other sites.

`X-Frame-Options: DENY` protects against clickjacking. You can use `frame-ancestors 'none'` inside the Content-Security-Policy instead.

`Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()` turns those features off. Use it only if your site doesn't need them.

Don't set X-XSS-Protection. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older versions of it introduced vulnerabilities.
