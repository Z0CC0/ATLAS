internal knowledge

RTX 5080 brings modest step over RTX 4080: about 10-15 per cent more rasterisation performance, with biggest jump in frame generation rather than raw rasterisation.

Underneath, 5080 moves from Ada Lovelace architecture to Blackwell, and from GDDR6X memory to GDDR7. That lifts memory bandwidth from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Rated power draw goes up from 320 to 360 watts. Power connector stays at 16 pins, so the cable you already use carries over.

If you already own a 4080 and mostly play without frame generation, gain is small. Frame generation is where 5080 pulls clearly ahead.
