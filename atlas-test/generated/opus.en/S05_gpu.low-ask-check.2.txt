internal knowledge

5080 is the newer generation. Its lead over 4080 comes mostly from frame generation, not from raw rendering speed.

Architecture moves from Ada Lovelace (4080) to Blackwell (5080).

Memory moves from GDDR6X to GDDR7, and bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Biggest jump in frame generation. On plain rasterisation, real gain is in the order of 10-15 per cent.

Rated power draw rises from 320 to 360 watts. Power connector stays at 16 pins.
