Before rewrite, check these:

**What actually changes.** Map which parts of monolith change often. Stable parts gain nothing from split — leave them.

**Transaction boundaries.** Where do transactions sit now? One spanning two future services become distributed transaction problem. Painful.

**Skip parallel rewrite.** Running new system alongside old, built from scratch, almost always fails — old system keep changing, copy never catch up.

**Strangle instead.** Router in front, move one piece at a time, old system stays authoritative until new piece verified. This work, parallel rewrite doesn't.

**Microservices cost operations, not code.** Each service want own releases, monitoring, logs, alerts, and someone who own it. Below 5 developers, operational cost almost always exceeds benefit.

**Modular monolith — nobody mentions it.** Sharp internal boundaries, one release, one deploy. Extract a module later, day you genuinely need to, not before.

**Real question isn't technical.** Ask: what must team do in a year that it can't do today? Answer decides architecture, not fashion.
