RTX 5080 belongs to newer generation: Blackwell architecture, where RTX 4080 uses Ada Lovelace. Biggest jump comes in frame generation, not raw rasterisation.

On rasterisation, real gain only in the order of 10-15 per cent over 4080.

Memory moves from GDDR6X to GDDR7, and bandwidth goes from about 717 to about 960 gigabytes per second.

Rated power draw rises from 320 to 360 watts, while power connector stays at 16 pins.
