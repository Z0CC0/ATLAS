The 5080 is a modest step up over the 4080 for most games. Its biggest gain is in frame generation, while raw rasterisation improves only about 10-15 per cent.

Architecture: Blackwell on the 5080, Ada Lovelace on the 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 on the 5080, GDDR6X on the 4080. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Power: rated draw rises from 320 to 360 W. Both cards use the same 16-pin connector.
