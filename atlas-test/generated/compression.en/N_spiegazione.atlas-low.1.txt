Eventual consistency: after write, replicas don't agree immediately, but converge in finite time once writes stop.

Trade: against availability. System demanding strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas can't talk to each other.

Matters right after you write: you post a comment, reload, comment not there yet.

Fix: route the session that just wrote to the primary replica, not everyone.

Convergence: under a second same region, seconds across regions.

Not a defect — a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Exceptions where unacceptable: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
