Find which parts of the monolith actually change first — stable parts gain nothing from splitting. Map transaction boundaries: a transaction spanning two services becomes a distributed problem. Skip a full rewrite running parallel to the old one — old system keeps changing, copy never catches up. Strangle instead: router in front, move one piece at a time, old stays authoritative until new is verified. Microservices cost more in ops — each service wants releases, monitoring, logs, alerts, an owner. Under five devs, that cost usually beats the benefit. Modular monolith is the option nobody mentions: sharp internal boundaries, one release, extract a module the day it's genuinely needed. Real question isn't technical: what must the team do in a year that it can't do today.
