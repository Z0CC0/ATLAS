2008: paper signed Satoshi Nakamoto, pseudonym, never identified. Title: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System." Problem: double spending without a central ledger-keeper. Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. PoW cost: rewriting the past means redoing every block since — expensive by design.

First block: 3 Jan 2009. Contains a Times headline on a bank bailout — timestamp and statement of intent both.

Issuance: reward starts 50 BTC, halves every 210,000 blocks (~4yr). Total tends to 21 million, no more.

2010: first documented commercial transaction — two pizzas, 10,000 BTC.

2011: Satoshi stops posting publicly. Those early coins never moved since.

Origin: cypherpunk scene. Predecessors b-money, bit gold — neither solved ordering.
