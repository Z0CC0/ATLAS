For a static site, seven headers cover it:

- **Strict-Transport-Security**: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` — forces the browser to always use HTTPS.
- **Content-Security-Policy**: `default-src 'self'` — blocks third-party scripts from being injected.
- **X-Content-Type-Options**: `nosniff` — stops the browser from guessing content types.
- **Referrer-Policy**: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin` — keeps the full path from leaking to other sites.
- **X-Frame-Options**: `DENY` (or `frame-ancestors 'none'` in your CSP) — prevents clickjacking.
- **Permissions-Policy** — disable camera, microphone, and geolocation if you don't use them.

Leave out **X-XSS-Protection**: it's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older browser versions actually had vulnerabilities in how they handled it — so it does more harm than good if included.
