Postgres better choice, for now.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to roughly thousand jobs/minute, plenty for small project. Job sits in same transaction as data, so job never starts while matching write rolled back. Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, latency under millisecond, but one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence needs config too — without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis once volume genuinely demands it.
