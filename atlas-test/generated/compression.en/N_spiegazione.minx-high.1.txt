Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas don't agree immediately, but converge within finite time once writes stop.

Where it bites: reading right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, comment missing.

Common fix: reads from primary replica for session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Typical convergence: under a second across replicas in same region, seconds across regions.

Trade is against availability. Strong consistency means refusing requests whenever replicas can't talk.

Not a defect to eliminate but a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few, recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, identifier uniqueness.
