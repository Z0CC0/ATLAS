Eventual consistency: after write, replicas disagree briefly, but converge in finite time if writes stop.

Trade-off: against availability. Strong consistency forces system to refuse requests when replicas can't talk.

Matters here: you post comment, reload, don't see it. Read-after-write problem.

Fix: read from primary replica for session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Convergence time: same region, under a second. Cross-region, rises to seconds.

Not a defect — a choice. Most systems accept it deliberately to scale.

Unacceptable cases (few, recognisable): account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of identifier. There, strong consistency needed.
