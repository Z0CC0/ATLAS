2008, paper appear signed Satoshi Nakamoto, pseudonym never identified. Title: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System." Problem tackled: double spending without central body keeping ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks, ordered by proof of work. Proof of work make rewriting past expensive — attacker redo work for every later block too.

First block generated 3 January 2009, contains headline of Times article about bank bailout. Works both as timestamp and statement of intent.

Block reward start 50 bitcoin, halve every 210,000 blocks (roughly four years). Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction: 2010, two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stop participating publicly in 2011. Those bitcoin never moved since.

Project grows out of cypherpunk scene, after earlier attempts like b-money and bit gold — which didn't solve ordering problem.
