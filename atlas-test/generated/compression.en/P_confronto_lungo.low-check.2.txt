Rule that always holds: log write must not sit in critical path of request.

At thousand req/min, sensible choice: local files plus collector shipping logs elsewhere — app never waits on log write.

Files: zero cost, no dependency, but search means grep, rotation configured by hand or disk fills. Search across several machines doesn't exist — connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention, but pay by volume, and at thousand req/min volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure — personal data must be stripped before they go.

Database: search familiar, convenient, but writing logs into app's own database puts two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections real requests needed.
