5080 gains most in frame generation, not raw rasterisation, where it's only about 10-15 per cent faster.

Architecture: Blackwell on 5080, Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X, bandwidth up from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Rated power: 320 W on 4080, 360 W on 5080.

Power connector unchanged: still 16-pin.
