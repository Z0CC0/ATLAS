Bitcoin began with a 2008 paper, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto. That is a pseudonym, never identified.

Roots in cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts, b-money and bit gold, never solved ordering.

Problem: double spending without central body keeping ledger. Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting past costly, since work must be redone for every later block.

First block generated 3 January 2009. Contains Times headline about bank bailout, both timestamp and statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin, halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction in 2010: two pizzas for 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stops public participation in 2011. Those bitcoin never moved.
