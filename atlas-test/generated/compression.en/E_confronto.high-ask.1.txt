Postgres, for now.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` covers up to ~1000 jobs/min. Job sits in the same transaction as the data — can't start if the write rolls back. Persistence is free: process dies, queue's still there.

Redis wins on volume and latency (sub-ms), but needs AOF configured or a restart wipes the queue, plus it's another service to run and maintain.

Move to Redis only when volume actually demands it.
