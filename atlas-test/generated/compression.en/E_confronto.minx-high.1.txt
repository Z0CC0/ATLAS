**Postgres**, until volume genuinely demands Redis.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so it never starts while its matching write is rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, latency under a millisecond.

But Redis is one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Its persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
