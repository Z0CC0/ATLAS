Set these six headers and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

Strict-Transport-Security with a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds and includeSubDomains. This forces the browser onto HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy with default-src 'self'. This blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin. This keeps the full path from leaking to other sites.

X-Frame-Options: DENY, or frame-ancestors 'none' inside the Content-Security-Policy. Either one protects against clickjacking.

Permissions-Policy to turn off camera, microphone and geolocation if the site doesn't use them.

Don't set X-XSS-Protection. It's deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, and older versions of it introduced vulnerabilities.

```
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains
Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
X-Frame-Options: DENY
Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()
```
