2008, paper appear signed Satoshi Nakamoto — pseudonym, never identified. Title: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". Problem it tackle: double spending without central body keep ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Proof of work make rewriting past expensive — attacker redo work for every block after point of change, not just one.

3 January 2009: first block generate, contain headline of Times article about bank bailout. Reference work two way — timestamp, and statement of intent.

Reward starts 50 bitcoin, halve every 210,000 blocks (~4 years). Total issued tend to 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction: 2010, two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

2011: Satoshi stop public participation. Those early bitcoin never moved since.

Project grow out of cypherpunk scene, after earlier attempts — b-money, bit gold — that fail solve ordering problem.
