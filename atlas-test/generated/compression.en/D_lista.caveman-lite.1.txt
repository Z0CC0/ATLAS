For static site, set these headers:

**Strict-Transport-Security**: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` — forces browser onto HTTPS, at least one year max-age.

**Content-Security-Policy**: `default-src 'self'` — blocks third-party script injection.

**X-Content-Type-Options**: `nosniff` — stops browser guessing content type.

**Referrer-Policy**: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin` — full path doesn't leak to other sites.

**X-Frame-Options**: `DENY` (or `frame-ancestors 'none'` in your CSP) — against clickjacking.

**Permissions-Policy**: disable camera, microphone, geolocation if unused.

Skip **X-XSS-Protection**: deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, older versions actually introduced vulnerabilities.
