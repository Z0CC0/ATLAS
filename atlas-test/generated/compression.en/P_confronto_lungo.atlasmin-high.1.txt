Local files plus a shipper.

App writes to local files, zero cost, no dependency. Rotation and grep set up by hand or disk fills. No cross-machine search, must connect to each machine.

A collector reads those files and ships them elsewhere. App never waits on the write — that's the rule that always holds, log write can't sit in the request's critical path.

Skip managed service as primary: instant search, alerts, retention, but billed by volume, and at 1k req/min volume grows fast. Logs leave your infra, personal data must be stripped first. Fine as the collector's destination once stripped, not fine as the direct write target.

Skip the app's own database: search is convenient but logs compete with real requests for cache and connections. Under load logs fill what requests needed.
