2008: paper appear, signed Satoshi Nakamoto — pseudonym, never identified. Title: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". Problem it solve: double spending without central body keeping ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks, ordered by proof of work. Proof of work make rewriting past expensive — attacker must redo work for every later block too.

First block generated 3 January 2009, contains headline of Times article about bank bailout. Works two ways: timestamp, and statement of intent.

Block reward starts 50 bitcoin, halves every 210,000 blocks (roughly four years). Total issued tends toward 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction: 2010, two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stops public participation in 2011. Those early bitcoin never moved since.

Project grows out of cypherpunk scene, after earlier attempts like b-money and bit gold — none solved ordering problem.
