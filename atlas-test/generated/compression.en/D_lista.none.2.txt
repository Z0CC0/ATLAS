For a static site, here's what to set:

**Strict-Transport-Security** — `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`. This forces browsers onto HTTPS, and 31536000 seconds (one year) is the minimum you want for the max-age.

**Content-Security-Policy** — at minimum `default-src 'self'`. This is what actually blocks third-party script injection, so it's worth having even on a "just static HTML" site.

**X-Content-Type-Options** — `nosniff`. Stops the browser from guessing content types on your files.

**Referrer-Policy** — `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. Keeps the full URL path from leaking to other sites when someone clicks a link off your pages.

**Clickjacking protection** — either `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, or `frame-ancestors 'none'` folded into your CSP. Either works; you don't need both.

**Permissions-Policy** — turn off camera, microphone, and geolocation if your site doesn't use them. No reason to leave those APIs available to a page that never calls them.

**Skip X-XSS-Protection** — it's deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and in older browsers it actually introduced vulnerabilities, so leave it out entirely rather than setting it to anything.
