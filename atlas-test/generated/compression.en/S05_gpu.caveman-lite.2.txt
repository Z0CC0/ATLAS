Two GPUs differ across architecture, memory, power, and where gains actually show up.

RTX 5080 runs Blackwell architecture with GDDR7 memory; RTX 4080 runs Ada Lovelace with GDDR6X. Memory bandwidth rises from about 717 GB/s (4080) to about 960 GB/s (5080). Rated power draw goes from 320W to 360W. Power connector stays 16-pin on both.

On raw rasterisation, gain is modest — around 10-15%. Biggest jump is in frame generation, not rasterisation.
