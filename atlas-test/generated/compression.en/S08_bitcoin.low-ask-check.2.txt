2008, paper signed Satoshi Nakamoto appears — pseudonym never identified. Title "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". Problem tackled: double spending without central body keeping ledger. Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work — rewriting past expensive, since redone needed for every later block.

Grows out of cypherpunk scene, after b-money and bit gold — earlier attempts that didn't solve ordering.

3 January 2009, first block generated, contains headline of Times article about bank bailout — works both as timestamp and statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin, halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

2010, first documented commercial transaction: two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

2011, Satoshi stops taking part publicly. Those bitcoin never moved since.
