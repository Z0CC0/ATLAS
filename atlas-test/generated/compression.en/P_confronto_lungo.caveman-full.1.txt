At thousand requests/minute, use files plus collector shipping elsewhere. Rule that always hold: log write must never sit in critical path of request.

**Files**: zero cost, no dependency. But search means grep, rotation manual setup or disk fill. Across machines search doesn't exist, connect to each one.

**Managed service**: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. Pay by volume, and volume grow fast at your rate. Logs leave your infrastructure, so strip personal data before send.

**Database**: search familiar, convenient. But logs in application's own DB compete with real workload. Under load, logs fill caches and connections real requests need.

Your setup: write locally to files (cheap, no blocking), collector ships them elsewhere for search/retention. Application never wait on log write.
