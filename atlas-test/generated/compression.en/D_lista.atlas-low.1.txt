Strict-Transport-Security, max-age at least 31536000 seconds, includeSubDomains — forces HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy, default-src 'self' — blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff — stops browser guessing content type.

Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin — full path won't leak to other sites.

X-Frame-Options: DENY, or frame-ancestors 'none' in CSP — against clickjacking.

Permissions-Policy — switch off camera, microphone, geolocation if unused.

Skip X-XSS-Protection: deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, older versions introduced vulnerabilities.
