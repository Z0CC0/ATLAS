Local files plus a shipping collector.

Files: zero cost, no dependency. Grep for search, manual rotation, disk fills without it. No cross-machine search — connect to each box separately.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, retention automatic. Costs scale with volume — at 1k req/min that volume grows fast. Logs leave your infra, so strip personal data first.

Database: search is convenient, but logs and app data compete for the same connections and cache. Under load, logging starves the real requests.

At 1k req/min: write to local files, ship elsewhere with a collector. App never waits on the log write.

Rule that always holds: logging must never sit in the request's critical path.
