Bitcoin came out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold never solved the ordering of transactions.

2008: paper *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System* appears, signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified.

Problem it tackles: double spending with no central body keeping the ledger.

Solution: a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive, because the work would have to be redone for every later block.

3 January 2009: first block generated. It contains the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which works both as a timestamp and as a statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, never more.

2010: first documented commercial transaction, two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

2011: Satoshi stops taking part publicly. Those bitcoin have never been moved.
