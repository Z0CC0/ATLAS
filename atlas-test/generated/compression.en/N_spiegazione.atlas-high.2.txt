Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas disagree for a moment, converge within finite time once writes stop. Same-region, under a second. Cross-region, seconds.

The trade is against availability. Demand strong consistency, and the system must refuse requests whenever replicas can't reach each other.

Hits you specifically at read-after-write: post a comment, reload, it's not there. Fix: route that session's reads to the primary, not the whole userbase.

Not a bug — a deliberate choice, made by most systems that scale.

Unacceptable only in a few recognisable spots: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
