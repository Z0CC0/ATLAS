After a write, replicas disagree briefly, converge within finite time once writes stop.

Trade: availability. Strong consistency refuses requests when replicas can't reach each other.

Hits you right after your own write — post comment, reload, gone.

Fix: route that session's reads to primary, not everyone's.

Convergence same-region <1s, cross-region seconds.

Not a defect. Deliberate choice, most systems at scale.

Unacceptable: balances, seat availability, unique IDs.
