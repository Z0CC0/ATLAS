Cache stores the already-processed prefix; identical-start requests reuse it, skip reprocessing. Match is prefix-only — one differing char at the start, no reuse past it. So stable content first, variable content after, not reverse. Cache write costs more than normal read — first request pricier. Gain starts at second request, grows with turns. Pays off: long conversations, large stable context — system instructions, docs, tool defs. Doesn't pay off: single differing requests — pay the write, never read back. A per-turn changing line, e.g. a clock, kills reuse of everything after it.
