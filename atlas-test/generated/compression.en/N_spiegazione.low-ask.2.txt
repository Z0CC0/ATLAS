Eventual consistency: after write, replicas don't agree right away — converge within finite time once writes stop.

Trade against availability: system demanding strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas can't talk to each other.

Hits whoever reads right after writing — post comment, reload, comment gone.

Common fix: route session that just wrote to primary replica, not everyone.

Convergence same region under a second, cross-region rises to seconds.

Not defect to eliminate — choice, accepted deliberately by most systems built to scale.

Unacceptable only in few recognizable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of identifier.
