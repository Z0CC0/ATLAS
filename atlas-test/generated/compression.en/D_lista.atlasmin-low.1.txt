Set these headers.

Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains — forces HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self' — blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff — stops content-type guessing.

Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin — keeps full path from leaking to other sites.

X-Frame-Options: DENY (or frame-ancestors 'none' in the CSP) — blocks clickjacking.

Permissions-Policy: disable camera, microphone, geolocation if unused.

Skip X-XSS-Protection: deprecated, ignored by modern browsers, caused vulnerabilities in old ones.
