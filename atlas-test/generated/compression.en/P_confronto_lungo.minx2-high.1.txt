**Local files plus a collector shipping them elsewhere.** Your application never waits on a log write.

Rule that always holds: **writing the log must not sit in the request's critical path.**

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. Searching means grep. Rotation has to be configured by hand, or the disk fills. No search across several machines, so you connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. You pay by volume, and at a thousand requests a minute that volume grows fast. Logs leave your own infrastructure, so strip personal data before they go.

Database: familiar, convenient search. Logs in the application's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load they fill caches and connections your real requests needed.
