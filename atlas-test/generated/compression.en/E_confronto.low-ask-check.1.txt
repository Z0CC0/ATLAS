Postgres.

Small project, volume under ~1000 jobs/minute, SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles it. Job sits in same transaction as data — job can't start while matching write rolls back. Process dies, queue still there, persistence free.

Redis faster, sub-millisecond, handles far more volume — but one more service to run, monitor, upgrade, and persistence needs AOF configured or restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis when volume genuinely demands it, not before.
