File plus collector. App writes to local files, collector ships them elsewhere, app never waits on the write.

Files alone: free, no dependency, but grep and rotation manual, disk fills without it, and searching across machines means connecting to each one.

Managed service alone: instant search, alerts, retention, but you pay per volume, and at 1000 req/min volume grows fast — plus logs leave your infra, so strip personal data first.

Database: search convenient, but log writes compete with request traffic for cache and connections app needs.

Rule that always holds: log write must never sit in the request's critical path.
