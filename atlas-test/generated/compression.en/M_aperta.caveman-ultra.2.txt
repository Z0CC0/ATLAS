Before pick architecture:

Find which part of monolith actually change. Stable parts gain nothing from split.

Find where transaction boundaries sit. Transaction spanning two services become distributed problem.

Skip full rewrite in parallel — old system keep changing, copy never catch up, almost always fail.

Instead: progressive strangling. Router in front, move one piece at time, old system stay authoritative until new piece verified.

Microservices cost more ops — each service want own releases, monitoring, logs, alerts, owner. Below five developers, ops cost almost always exceed benefit.

Consider modular monolith: sharp internal boundaries, one release, extract module later if genuinely needed. Nobody mention this option but real.

Real deciding question not technical: what team must do in year from now that it cannot do today.
