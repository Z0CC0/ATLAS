Prompt caching stores the already-processed prefix of a request. Next request, same prefix — no reprocessing cost for that part.

Prefix-only: one different character at the start, no reuse past that point. So stable content goes first — system instructions, docs, tool defs — variable content after. Reverse the order and caching breaks.

First write costs more than a normal read. Gain starts second request, grows with turn count.

Pays off: long conversations, large stable context.

Doesn't pay off: single requests differing from each other — you pay the write, never read it back.

Gotcha: a changing line early (a clock, timestamp) kills reuse for everything after it.
