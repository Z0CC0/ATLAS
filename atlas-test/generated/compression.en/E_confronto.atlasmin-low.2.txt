Postgres, for small project.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to roughly thousand jobs per minute — plenty at small scale.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so job never starts while matching write rolls back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, latency under millisecond — but one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence needs config too: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis once volume genuinely demands it.
