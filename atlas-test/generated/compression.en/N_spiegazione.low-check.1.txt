Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas don't agree immediately — they converge in finite time if writes stop.

Trade-off is against availability. A system demanding strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas can't talk to each other.

Matters right after you write: you post a comment, reload, comment's not there yet.

Fix: read-your-writes — route the session that just wrote to the primary replica, not everyone.

Convergence: under a second same region, seconds across regions.

Not a defect, a choice — most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable cases, few and recognizable: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
