Postgres.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` covers up to ~1000 jobs/min, plenty for small scale. Job rides the same transaction as the data — write rolls back, job never fires. Persistence free: process dies, queue's still there.

Redis beats it on volume and latency, sub-millisecond — but it's another service to run, monitor, upgrade, and persistence isn't automatic: no AOF, restart wipes the queue.

Move to Redis when volume actually demands it, not before.
