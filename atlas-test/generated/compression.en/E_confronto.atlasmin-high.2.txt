Postgres.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to ~1000 jobs/min, enough for a small project. Job lives in same transaction as data — rolled-back write means job never fires. Process dies, queue survives, no config needed.

Redis wins on volume and latency (sub-ms) but costs a service to run/monitor/upgrade, and needs AOF configured or a restart wipes queued jobs.

Move to Redis when volume actually demands it.
