Before rewrite, sort few things:

**What actually changes.** Find which parts of monolith see real churn. Stable parts gain nothing from split — don't touch them.

**Transaction boundaries.** Map where transactions sit now. A transaction spanning two services becomes distributed problem — expensive one.

**Skip parallel rewrite.** Building new system alongside old almost always fails: old keeps changing, copy never catches up.

**Use strangler pattern instead.** Router in front, move one piece at a time, keep old system authoritative until new piece verified.

**Microservices cost ops, not just code.** Each service wants own releases, monitoring, logs, alerts, owner. Below five developers, that ops cost usually exceeds benefit.

**Consider modular monolith.** Sharp internal boundaries, one release, but option to extract module later when genuinely needed. Underrated middle ground.

**Real question isn't technical.** Ask: what must team do in year that it can't do today? Answer decides architecture, not tech preference.
