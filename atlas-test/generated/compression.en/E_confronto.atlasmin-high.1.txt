Postgres.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to ~1000 jobs/min, enough for small-project volume.

Job lives in same transaction as data — write rolls back, job never starts.

Persistence free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis wins on volume (much higher) and latency (sub-ms), but adds a service to run/monitor/upgrade, and persistence needs AOF configured — without it, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.
