Files, shipped elsewhere by a collector.

At a thousand requests a minute: local files, collector ships them out, app never waits on the write.

Bare files break down once you have several machines: no cross-machine search, each one grep'd separately, rotation configured by hand or disk fills.

Managed service gives instant search, alerts, automatic retention, but volume costs money at this rate and personal data must be stripped before it leaves your infrastructure.

Database competes with your real workload for the same caches and connections under load — logs and requests shouldn't share that.

Rule that always holds: log write must never sit in the request's critical path. Files + collector satisfies it; database and synchronous shipping to a managed service don't.
