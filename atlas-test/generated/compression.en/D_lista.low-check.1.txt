Set these on your static site:

Strict-Transport-Security, max-age at least 31536000 seconds plus includeSubDomains — forces HTTPS.
Content-Security-Policy with default-src 'self' — blocks third-party script injection.
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff — stops MIME-type guessing.
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin — keeps full path from leaking to other sites.
X-Frame-Options: DENY, or frame-ancestors 'none' in the CSP — against clickjacking.
Permissions-Policy — switch off camera, microphone, geolocation if unused.

Skip X-XSS-Protection: deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, older versions introduced vulnerabilities.
