Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas disagree for a while, then converge within finite time once writes stop.

Trade is against availability. Strong consistency means refusing requests whenever replicas can't talk to each other.

It affects whoever reads right after writing. User posts a comment, reloads, and doesn't see it.

Common fix: read from the primary replica for the session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Typical convergence: under a second within one region, seconds across regions.

**Not a defect to eliminate, but a choice.** Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in a few recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
