Eventual consistency: after write, replicas disagree briefly, converge if writes stop. Same region under 1 second, cross-region seconds.

Trade: against availability. Strong consistency system must refuse requests when replicas can't talk.

Hits you right after write — post comment, reload, comment gone. Common fix: route that session's reads to primary replica (only for user who just wrote, not everyone).

Not defect, choice. Most systems at scale pick it deliberately.

Unacceptable cases, few and clear: account balances, seat availability, unique identifiers. There, need strong consistency instead.
