Replicas don't agree right after a write. They converge within finite time once writes stop.

Why care: user posts comment, reloads, doesn't see it. It hits whoever reads right after writing.

Fix: read from primary replica for session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Convergence typically under a second across replicas in same region; seconds across regions.

Trade is against availability: system demanding strong consistency must refuse requests when replicas cannot talk.

Not defect to eliminate but choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Unacceptable only in few recognisable cases: account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
