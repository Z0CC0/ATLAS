**Local files plus a collector that ships them elsewhere.** The application never waits on a log write.

Rule that always holds: writing the log must not sit in the request's critical path.

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. Search means grep. Rotation must be configured by hand, or the disk fills. No search across several machines; you connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. You pay by volume, and at a thousand requests a minute the volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure, so strip personal data before they go.

Database: familiar, convenient search. But logs in the application's own database put two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections the real requests needed.
