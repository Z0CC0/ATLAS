Postgres, for small project.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so job can never start while matching write gets rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond. Cost: one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Its persistence also needs configuring. Without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis when volume genuinely demands it.
