2008: paper appear, signed Satoshi Nakamoto — pseudonym, never identified. Title: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". Problem it solve: double spending without central body keep ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks, ordered by proof of work. Proof of work make rewrite past expensive — attacker redo work for every later block too.

First block: 3 January 2009. Contain headline of Times article about bank bailout. Work double duty: timestamp, and statement of intent.

Reward: start 50 bitcoin, halve every 210,000 blocks — roughly four years. Total issued tend toward 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction: 2010, two pizzas for 10,000 bitcoin. Satoshi stop public participation 2011. Those bitcoin never move since.

Origin: grow out of cypherpunk scene, after earlier attempts — b-money, bit gold — that not solve ordering problem.
