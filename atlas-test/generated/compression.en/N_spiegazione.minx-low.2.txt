Eventual consistency: after a write, replicas don't agree immediately, but converge within finite time once writes stop.

Price paid for availability. System demanding strong consistency has to refuse requests when replicas cannot talk to each other.

Why you care: it bites whoever reads right after writing. User posts comment, reloads, doesn't see it.

Common fix: read from primary replica for session that just wrote, not for everyone.

Convergence time: typically under a second across replicas in same region, seconds across regions.

Not a defect to eliminate but a choice. Most systems that scale accept it deliberately.

Cases where it's unacceptable: few and easy to recognise. Account balances, seat availability, uniqueness of an identifier.
