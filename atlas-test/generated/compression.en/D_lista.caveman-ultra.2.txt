Static site, add these headers:

**Strict-Transport-Security**: `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` — force HTTPS.

**Content-Security-Policy**: `default-src 'self'` — block third-party script injection.

**X-Content-Type-Options**: `nosniff` — stop MIME-type guessing.

**Referrer-Policy**: `strict-origin-when-cross-origin` — full path not leak to other sites.

**X-Frame-Options**: `DENY` (or `frame-ancestors 'none'` in CSP) — clickjacking protection.

**Permissions-Policy**: disable camera, microphone, geolocation if unused.

Skip **X-XSS-Protection** — deprecated, modern browsers ignore it, old versions actually introduced vulnerabilities.
