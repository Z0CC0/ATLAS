Postgres. Small project, SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles roughly thousand jobs per minute — plenty at this scale. Job sits in same transaction as data, so job never starts while matching write rolled back. Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, latency under millisecond, but one more service to run, monitor, upgrade — and persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis when volume genuinely demands it.
