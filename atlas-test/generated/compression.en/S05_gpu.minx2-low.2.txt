RTX 5080 is a newer generation with a modest rasterisation gain. Its biggest jump over the 4080 is in frame generation.

Architecture: Blackwell on 5080, Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X, bandwidth up from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Performance: real rasterisation gain in the order of 10-15 per cent.

Power: rated draw rises from 320 to 360 W. Connector stays 16-pin.
