Big rewrite question. Consider this:

Know which part of monolith actually change. Stable parts gain nothing from split — don't touch them.

Find where transaction boundaries sit. Transaction spanning two services become distributed problem — hard, avoid if possible.

Skip full parallel rewrite. Old system keep changing, copy never catch up, almost always fail.

Use progressive strangling instead: router in front, move one piece at time, old system stay authoritative until new piece verified.

Microservices cost more in ops: every service want own releases, monitoring, logs, alerts, and someone to own it. Below five developers, that cost usually beats the benefit.

Modular monolith is option most skip: sharp internal boundaries, one release, still can extract module later when genuinely needed.

Real question not technical: what must team do in year that it can't do today. Architecture decision follow from that answer.
