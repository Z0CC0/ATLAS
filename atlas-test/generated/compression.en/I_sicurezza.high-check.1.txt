No. Anything sent to the browser can be read — devtools, network tab, obfuscation doesn't survive either.

Key stays on the server. Frontend calls your own backend endpoint, that endpoint calls the service.

Rate-limit that endpoint, or it's a free tap on your key.

If the key's already out there, it's compromised — rotate it now, don't just delete it from the code. Removing it from a commit leaves it in git history; anyone with a clone still has it.
