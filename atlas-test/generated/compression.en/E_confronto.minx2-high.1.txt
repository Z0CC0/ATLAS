**Postgres.** Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job shares transaction with data: job never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis: far more volume, lower latency, under a millisecond. But one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
