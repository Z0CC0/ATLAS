Use Postgres.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute, plenty for small project. Job lives in same transaction as your data, so job can never start when matching write rolled back. Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis wins on volume and latency (sub-millisecond), but costs you: one more service to run, monitor, upgrade. Persistence not automatic either — without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.

Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.
