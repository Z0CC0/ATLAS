Set these six headers, and leave out X-XSS-Protection.

Strict-Transport-Security with a max-age of at least 31536000 seconds and includeSubDomains. This forces the browser onto HTTPS.

Content-Security-Policy with default-src 'self'. This is the header that blocks third-party script injection.

X-Content-Type-Options set to nosniff. This stops the browser from guessing the content type.

Referrer-Policy set to strict-origin-when-cross-origin. This keeps your full path from leaking to other sites.

X-Frame-Options set to DENY, or frame-ancestors 'none' inside your Content-Security-Policy. Either one protects against clickjacking.

Permissions-Policy turning off camera, microphone and geolocation, if your site doesn't use them.

**Do not set X-XSS-Protection.** It is deprecated. Modern browsers ignore it, and older browser versions that support it introduced vulnerabilities.
