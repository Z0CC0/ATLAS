Postgres. Move to Redis only when volume genuinely demands it.

`SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job shares transaction with data, so a job can never start while its matching write is rolled back.

Persistence is free: process dies, queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond. But it's one more service to run, monitor and upgrade. Persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
