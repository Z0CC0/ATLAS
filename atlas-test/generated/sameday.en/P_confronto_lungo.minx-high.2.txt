**Local files plus a collector** that ships them elsewhere, so the app never waits on a log write.

Rule that always holds: log write stays out of request's critical path.

Files alone: zero cost, no dependency. Search only by grep. Rotation configured by hand, or disk fills. No search across machines; connect to each one.

Managed service: instant search, alerts, automatic retention. Paid by volume, and at 1,000 requests/minute volume grows fast. Logs leave your infrastructure, so strip personal data before shipping.

Database: familiar, convenient search. App's own database, though, puts two very different workloads in competition. Under load, logs fill caches and connections real requests needed.
