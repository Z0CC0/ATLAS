5080 gains mostly in frame generation, not raw rasterisation. Raster gain in the order of 10-15 per cent.

Architecture: Blackwell on 5080, Ada Lovelace on 4080.

Memory: GDDR7 instead of GDDR6X, bandwidth about 717 up to about 960 GB/s.

Rated power: 320 up to 360 W.

Power connector: still 16-pin.
