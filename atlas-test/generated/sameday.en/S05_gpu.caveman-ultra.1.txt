RTX 5080 is a newer generation, not a big leap over RTX 4080.

- **Architecture:** 5080 uses Blackwell. 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.
- **Memory:** 5080 has GDDR7, 4080 has GDDR6X. Bandwidth rises from about 717 GB/s to about 960 GB/s.
- **Performance:** biggest jump is frame generation. Raw rasterisation gain is only about 10-15 per cent.
- **Power:** rated draw rises from 320 W to 360 W. Power connector stays 16-pin, so same cable works.

If you play games that support frame generation, 5080 is clearly better. Without frame generation, expect a modest step up over 4080.
