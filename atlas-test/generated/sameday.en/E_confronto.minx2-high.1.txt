Postgres, and move to Redis when volume genuinely demands it.

Postgres with `SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job sits in same transaction as data, so job never starts while matching write rolled back.

Persistence free: process dies, queue still there.

Redis handles far more volume, latency under a millisecond.

Redis costs one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, restart loses queued jobs.
