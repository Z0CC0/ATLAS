Bitcoin grew out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts, b-money and bit gold, never solved ordering.

2008: paper signed Satoshi Nakamoto, pseudonym never identified. Title: "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". Problem: double spending without central body keeping ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting past is expensive, since work must be redone for every later block.

First block generated 3 January 2009. Contains headline of Times article on bank bailout. Serves as both timestamp and statement of intent.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin, halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

First documented commercial transaction in 2010: two pizzas for 10,000 bitcoin.

Satoshi stops public participation in 2011. Those bitcoin never moved.
