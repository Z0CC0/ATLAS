**Postgres**, then switch to Redis when volume truly needs it.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job shares a transaction with its data, so it never starts when the matching write rolls back.

Persistence comes free: if the process dies, the queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume, with latency under a millisecond. But it's one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF enabled, a restart loses queued jobs.
