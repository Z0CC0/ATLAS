Bitcoin came out of the cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts such as b-money and bit gold had not solved how to order transactions.

2008: a paper appears, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto. The pseudonym has never been identified.

The paper tackles double spending without a central body keeping the ledger. Its solution is a chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting the past is expensive because the work would have to be redone for every later block.

3 January 2009: the first block is generated. It holds the headline of a Times article about a bank bailout, which serves as both a timestamp and a statement of intent.

The block reward starts at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. The total issued approaches 21 million and never goes past it.

2010: the first documented commercial transaction, two pizzas paid for with 10,000 bitcoin.

2011: Satoshi stops taking part publicly. Their bitcoin have never been moved.
