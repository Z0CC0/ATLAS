**Postgres**, moving to Redis only when volume really requires it.

SELECT FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED handles up to roughly a thousand jobs per minute.

Job shares the transaction with its data, so a job never starts while its write is rolled back.

Persistence comes free: if the process dies, the queue survives.

Redis handles far more volume at lower latency, under a millisecond, but it's one more service to run, monitor and upgrade.

Redis persistence needs configuring: without AOF, a restart loses queued jobs.
