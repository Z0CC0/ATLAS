Biggest jump is frame generation, not raw rasterisation. On rasterisation, real gain is roughly 10-15 per cent.

5080 uses Blackwell architecture; 4080 uses Ada Lovelace.

Memory moves from GDDR6X to GDDR7. Bandwidth rises from about 717 to about 960 GB/s.

Rated power draw rises from 320 W to 360 W. Power connector stays at 16 pins.
