Bitcoin began with a 2008 paper, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", signed Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym never identified.

Roots in cypherpunk scene. Earlier attempts, b-money and bit gold, did not solve ordering.

Problem tackled: double spending without central body keeping ledger.

Solution: chain of blocks ordered by proof of work. Rewriting past expensive, since work must be redone for every later block.

3 January 2009: first block generated, containing Times headline about bank bailout. Works as timestamp and statement of intent both.

Block reward starts at 50 bitcoin, halves every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. Total issued tends to 21 million, no further.

2010: first documented commercial transaction, two pizzas paid with 10,000 bitcoin.

2011: Satoshi stops taking part publicly. Satoshi's bitcoin never moved.
