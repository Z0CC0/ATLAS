---
description: Places to read about a topic, not an answer
---

Use the `atlas-research` subagent in its **sources** shape: it comes back with where to go and
read — videos, discussions, articles, grouped by kind, one line each on what is in it and why it
is worth opening — not with the answer.

For the answer instead, that is `atlas-search`. Typing one of the two is what decides the shape,
so neither has to be worked out and neither asks.
