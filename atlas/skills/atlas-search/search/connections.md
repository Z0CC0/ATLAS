# Connections for search

Read when someone asks to reach what `atlas-search` cannot. None is installed for you: show the steps and the disadvantages, and let the person decide. Never ask for a key or a password in the chat.

## Accounts and keys of your own

Bluesky, Reddit, YouTube and X offer access to whoever registers, and their terms and prices change, so read them first. The key goes in a file on your machine, never in a chat.

- **Advantages:** real results from networks a stranger cannot read.
- **Disadvantages:** a registration each, limits on how much you can read, and on some of them a price. Not tried here.

## Your browser, already logged in

`atlas-browser` or a browser MCP can read what you can read.

- **Advantages:** it reaches X, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn as you see them.
- **Disadvantages:** slow; automating a network can break its terms and put your account at risk; and posts are written by strangers, some of them to be read by a model. Use it for one question at a time. Not tried here for this purpose.

## What this plugin will not do

Take cookies out of a browser, or get round a refusal.
